💡 Important Limitation

BMI does not distinguish between muscle and fat. Athletes and bodybuilders often have high BMIs despite having low body fat. Always use BMI alongside waist circumference and professional medical advice for a complete health picture.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is BMI accurate for everyone?
No. BMI can be misleading for muscular athletes (who may be classified as overweight), older adults (who may carry more fat at a normal BMI), and certain ethnic groups who face health risks at lower BMI values.
What is a healthy BMI for women vs. men?
The standard BMI scale applies to both men and women: under 18.5 is underweight, 18.5–24.9 is normal, 25–29.9 is overweight, and 30+ is obese. However, women naturally carry more essential body fat than men at the same BMI.
What should I do if my BMI is high?
Consult your doctor first. They can assess whether your BMI is a genuine health concern and recommend sustainable lifestyle changes. Small, consistent adjustments to diet and exercise typically produce the best long-term results.
Can children use this BMI calculator?
This calculator is designed for adults (20+). Children's BMI is calculated differently, using age and sex-specific percentile charts. Please consult a pediatrician for a child's weight assessment.
