How to Actually Stick to a Savings Goal

Setting a savings goal is easy. Hitting it is where most people fall short — studies consistently show that vague goals like "save more" rarely succeed, while specific, time-bound goals with automatic transfers succeed far more often.

The single most effective tactic is automating the transfer the day you get paid, before you have a chance to spend the money. People who manually move money at month-end save dramatically less than people who automate it, simply because willpower is a depleting resource and automation removes the decision entirely.

Break large goals into milestones. A $10,000 emergency fund goal feels distant and demotivating; "$833/month for 12 months" feels achievable and trackable. Visualizing progress — even a simple spreadsheet bar — measurably increases follow-through.

Use the calculator above to find your required monthly contribution, then set up an automatic transfer for that exact amount the day after each payday. Small, automatic, and boring beats large, manual, and motivated almost every time.
