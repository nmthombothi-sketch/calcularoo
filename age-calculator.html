Frequently Asked Questions

How does this handle leap years?
If you were born on February 29th, the calculator accurately accounts for the extra day. In non-leap years, it calculates your birthday as March 1st for legal and mathematical precision, matching standard calendar logic.
Is my date of birth stored or tracked?
Absolutely not. All calculations happen instantly in your web browser using JavaScript. We never collect, store, or transmit your date of birth or any other personal data.
Can I calculate the age between two specific dates?
This tool is optimized for calculating your age from birth to today. If you need to calculate the duration between two historical dates, check out our Date Range Calculator.
